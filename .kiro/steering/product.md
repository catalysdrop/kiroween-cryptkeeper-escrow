# Product — CryptKeeper Escrow

## What it is

A haunted escrow desk for freelance and bounty deals. A buyer locks
payment in a smart contract; a seller submits delivery evidence; an AI
agent — the Crypt Keeper — verifies the evidence and the contract
releases or refunds the money. No human middleman.

## Who it is for

- Freelancers and clients who do not trust each other yet.
- Bounty hunters and sponsors (the author's own world: bug bounties,
  hackathons, agent work).
- Hackathon judges who need to evaluate the whole flow in under a
  minute — hence demo mode with no wallet.

## Key features

1. On-chain escrow (Solidity, native BNB) with a strict state machine.
2. Deterministic, explainable AI verdicts — every check is listed with
   its result; no black-box scoring.
3. Demo mode: full flow in the browser, no wallet, no chain.
4. Spooky crypt theme that serves the function (deal states are stages
   of a haunting, each with a plain-language explanation).

## Tone

Playful-haunted, never gory. The Keeper is a witty grave-keeper, not a
horror villain. All user-facing copy is plain English; crypto jargon is
explained where it appears.
